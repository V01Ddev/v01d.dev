Since we know that $E_k=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ and momentum is $p=mv$, we can derive an equation for energy.
\begin{align*}
    v&=\frac{p}{m} \\\\
    E_k&=\frac{1}{2}mv^2 \\\\
    E_k&=\frac{1}{2}m\Big(\frac{p}{m}\Big)^2 \\\\
    E_k&=\frac{1}{2}m\frac{p^2}{m^2} \\\\
    E_k&=\frac{1}{2} \frac{p^2}{m} \\\\
    E_k&=\frac{p^2}{2m}
\end{align*}
