Why?

It was a life long dream to create a plane and although you can make a glider with cheap foam board and hot glue. Controlling it was a different challenge. The cheapest available RC controller locally available to me would have cost no less than 100 USD.

I spent most of my childhood dreaming of the day I can watch my own aircraft soar while obsessing over channels such as Flitetest.
