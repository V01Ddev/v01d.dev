How I Made a ESP32 Jamming PCB

August 20, 2026

Introduction

As much as I love solder wires manually onto perfboard. It quickly becomes unrealistic to maintain projects. Don’t get me wrong, a quick prototype is fine. But try try soldering 3 SPI components to an ESP and there is no way of avoiding a rats nest of a board.

The Goal

My main objective was to recreate the ESP32 + NRF24 jamming board from EmenstaNougat at home. With full support for their firmware in addition to allowing me to use known working hardware to develop my own jamming software for educational purposes strictly! Of course.
